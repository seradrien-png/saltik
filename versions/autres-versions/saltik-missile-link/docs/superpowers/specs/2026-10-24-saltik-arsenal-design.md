# Saltik arsenal, evasion and enemy encounter update

## Intent and authorization

Make combat legible, physical and varied: realistic blasts, four recognizable evasions, guided missiles that navigate cover and hit visible enemies, a visibly elevating main cannon, a short primary reload, a green enemy Saltik with two attacks, and a materially varied canyon. The user explicitly requested agents and autonomous delivery. Root approves routine choices, owns final visual review, assets, browser verification and publication. This design supersedes earlier PLAN/depth and repeated-three-route requirements.

## Approach

Chosen: retain the fixed-step world-coordinate engine and existing native player identity, add small pure combat modules, and integrate through explicit geometry and collision contracts. This keeps the accepted 0.60 s native turn, melee, movement, drones and terminal boss. A skeletal/physics rewrite would multiply regression risk; an effect-only patch would leave the collision, route and weapon failures unresolved.

## Required behavior

- Replace every visible PLAN label with ESQUIVE. Keep center pad, F and Shift input; its internal `shift` action may remain for compatibility. Remove depth displacement, depth immunity, rear-lane masking and translucent/duplicate player imagery.
- A rising dodge edge selects one of `tilt`, `spin`, `deflect`, `burrow` using the game seed, weighted by incoming projectile kinds/trajectories and grounded state. Each move has its own painted 4-frame row, active collision window and recovery. Deflect redirects one compatible projectile then shows a sore-arm shake. Burrow is a rapid partial immersion on real support; spin visibly lifts/rotates; tilt visibly balances on two lateral legs. There is no blanket immunity for the full animation. Weapons and melee lock during evasion; cooldown and release-to-reuse remain.
- Player guided salvos launch from the three measured native pod mouths, initially along those mouths. Acquire living visible enemies (including the boss, excluding crates); show red lock brackets only on targets assigned to live player missiles. Navigate physical obstacles and route slabs using steering and waypoints. Engine swept collision remains authoritative; missiles never teleport, tunnel through cover or obtain artificial target hits. Three missiles use distinct approach sides/impact offsets even against one enemy. Reacquire living visible targets when an assigned target dies.
- MIRV input starts a visible 0.24 s cannon elevation before launch. The native roof barrel is isolated/rotated around its measured pivot; there is no extra drawn gun. The measured aperture, flash and projectile velocity use the same cannon direction. Existing carrier/cluster behavior and recharge remain after launch. Turning or evasion cancels unlaunched preparation without spending recharge.
- Primary fire accumulates exactly 5.0 s of active firing intervals. Holding while turning, dodging, paused, dead or reloading spends nothing. Release preserves accumulated firing time. At threshold begin 0.70 s reload, stop primary shots, play distinct start/finish sounds, and show primary progress/status. Taps during reload are discarded; no delayed shot appears after a released tap. A continuously held trigger resumes once ready. Other weapons keep their independent timing.
- Green/cream/orange enemy Saltik has two cyan eyes, four wheel legs and visibly raised articulated square rocket pods. Replace three existing heavy scheduled encounters, keeping encounter count bounded. It alternates a short aimed gun burst with a visible missile lock and a pair of locking missiles. Hostile missiles are physical, collide with cover, and can be intercepted by player bullets or player blasts. Enemy geometry owns its bounds and actual gun/pod apertures.
- Route topology genuinely changes between one, two and three walkable lanes along the run. Ground-only stretches and single split passages must be meaningful, with brief three-path crossings. Earth, stone, vegetation, reclaimed buildings and wreckage change silhouette and support structures; prevent broad rectangular stacked facades and repeated parallel waves. Preserve real support, reachable climbs, useful cover, no countdown and ordinary survival/victory in 75–110 s.
- Explosions use native fire/smoke atlas animation plus light, pressure, sparks and drifting smoke. Remove square confetti from explosion events. Preserve camera/world registration and bound effect lifetime/count.

## Shared technical rules

All positions, bounds, apertures and trajectories use world units; only rendering subtracts camera. Fixed simulation step is 1/60 s, capped at 1/30. The same normalized target bounds feed aim, guidance, reticles and swept collision. Engine owns position integration, earliest collision fraction, damage and projectile removal. Modules do not bypass collision. Rendering follows simulation state rather than maintaining its own combat timers. `game.viewport={x,y,w,h}` is supplied by main from renderer; headless engine has a deterministic forward 1280×720 default. Target visibility requires intersection with this rectangle with a small 40-unit margin, not merely distance from player.

New asset keys are `saltik-evasion` (1254×1254, 4×4 nominal313.5px cells), `enemy-saltik` (1536×1024, 4×2), and `combat-blast` (root supplies dimensions/layout metadata before renderer finalization). Evasion rows are tilt, spin-jump, hand-deflect/arm-shake, partial burrow; each has anticipation, action, action/recovery, recovery. Enemy frames0–5 are idleL, gunL, walkingL1, walkingL2, podraiseL and open-podlaunchL; frame7 is nativeidleR. Frame6 is unused due to malformed generated anatomy. Runtime WebP encoding preserves dimensions, alpha and every visible RGBA pixel; PNG masters remain outside final runtime bundle. Root supplies native RGBA sheets; workers measure final alpha rather than assuming equal painted margins.

## Verification and limits

Pure tests cover seeded move compatibility/windows, navigation around blocking cover, distinct approaches, target death, enemy attack timing and geometry. Integration tests cover same-frame interception ordering, dodge collision fractions, visible-only acquisition, real cannon launch, 5 s reload accounting and input discard. Retire PLAN and fixed-six-upper-section assertions. Run the whole existing suite and ordinary campaign; root reviews desktop/mobile visuals, each dodge, lock brackets, cannon, enemy attacks, explosions and route silhouettes before private publication.

## Delivered refinements

Primary fire leads a visible approaching hostile missile only when a real clear shot exists; it does not select off-screen threats. Ground-only tilt/burrow cancel immediately if a jump, drop or edge removes support. The vertical camera retains HUD clearance during a spin-jump from the highest roof. Native hand recovery isolates one hand/forearm rather than shaking the hull. Decorative rear buttresses follow the actual sloped cap and omit pasted rectangular plating; their geometry does not change physical support or projectile collision.
