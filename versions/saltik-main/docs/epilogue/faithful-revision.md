# Faithful-design revision

User rejected the original film's redesigned Striker/carrier and missing green/orange Saltik. New edit renders canonical Striker, green/orange and DR-01 with the exact Matin Doré drawing functions, native sprite geometry and material baking. Their art is never regenerated. Two missile cutouts were extracted with image_gen from the two reference images reattached by the user: blue/lime has six missiles and round radar dish; navy/red has eight missiles, rectangular radar and red cobra marks. The resulting source is scripts/faithful-film/assets/missile-pair.png; validate visually against those attachments, not the different 3D variants stored in Drive.

36-second 1280×720/30fps edit: native Striker, DR-01, green/orange, paired missile specialists, captive siblings, opening gate and five-character reunion, then full-screen SALTIK / by limule & régalec at 29.5 seconds. Original story narration and score retained, followed at 32 seconds by a clearly separate synthetic commercial voice saying exactly “More to come on your smartphone.” The advertising cue uses ffmpeg Flite/rms with EQ and compression, no cloned voice. Cinematic treatment uses letterboxing, native animation, compositing, dolly moves, gate motion, dust, vignette and short shutter transitions.

Render source and exporter: scripts/faithful-film/preview.html, preview.js, export.py. Inputs original soundtrack and commercial.wav are retained in the source directory for reproduction. No gameplay logic, controls or character artwork is changed. Same lazy victory-film controller and public V2 Site.

To reproduce, serve the repository root with `python -m http.server 4174 --bind 127.0.0.1`, then run `python scripts/faithful-film/export.py`. Requires Python Playwright, Chromium at /usr/bin/chromium and ffmpeg. The exporter initializes its external output directory. The English cards are editorial story statements rather than verbatim closed captions; the retained narration says “We will bring them home” while the card explicitly states their willingness to fight for liberation.

Independent source/frame review found no Critical/Important issue. Finished media and browser lifecycle are checked separately. Original user attachments remain the visual reference for the extracted missile characters; their generated extraction is not asserted to be pixel-identical to the source photographs.
