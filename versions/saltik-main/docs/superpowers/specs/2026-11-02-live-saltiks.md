# Living Saltiks, approved design

User approved direct V2 integration of mechanical expressive animation for both Saltiks, with backup of current version. Preserve V1 Encre and original color V1. Preserve colors, controls, damage, target acquisition, reload durations, defensive cap and both QTE mechanics.

Use native 2D articulated pieces and cached textures, no video or 3D. Player/enemy move legs and wheel details, suspension follows terrain and motion; subtle hands/eye reflections without any mouth motion or extra limbs. Cannon follows credible bounded mechanical rotation/recoil. Guided missiles leave the visible used pod mouths, those slots stay empty during cooldown, fresh rounds mechanically feed back only at readiness. Keep three player missiles and two enemy missiles, with honest slot occupancy rather than filling every decorative hole.

Boss hull sequence: three effort presses tear open a hatch; keep that torn hatch caught at an edge so one existing small hand can use it as a brace. Saltik leans over the hole, plants/tucks wheels on finite roof, and aims by body inclination plus limited cannon elevation. Six firing pulses add short axial recoil and body reaction. No independent 90-degree barrel flailing, no stretched claw, no invented support. Existing pod action and safe ejection remain coherent.

Approach chosen: bounded native-patch articulation and deterministic cosmetic state driven from real launch/cooldown events; actual geometry shared with projectile emission. Reject large new sprite/video sheets and always-moving cosmetic effects that would obscure gameplay or undo missile optimization.
