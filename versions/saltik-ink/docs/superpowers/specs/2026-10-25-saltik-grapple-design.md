# Saltik controls and grapple encounter

Continue the existing game and publish to its current owner-private Site. Preserve native artwork, terrain, routes, music, drone/boss combat and normal input ownership.

The user specifies DÉVIER at the centre of the left pad and no DÉVIER button on the right. Place a separate ESQUIVE button just outside the lower-right corner of the pad, preserving all eight direction cells and at least 44px targets. H remains manual projectile deflection. F/Shift now always triggers a rearward balance: exactly 1.5s protected, with .2s anticipation and .2s recovery (1.9s total). No random spin selection on the relocated dodge control. Pad size/transparency and zoom remain.

Enemy Saltik becomes modestly tougher: HP24 instead of16. Ruling: 'réduite aux trois quarts' means75% remaining (18HP). Damage that crosses this threshold clamps to18, so an explosive cannot skip the requested encounter. At/below the threshold it visibly deflects or dodges every ranged attack and indicates APPROCHEZ. Contact at the same traversable support/height automatically begins a hands-locked strength contest. Ruling: the QTE needs five distinct presses of one simple red button (Space/Enter as keyboard alternatives), with no unrequested deadline. Held input, keyboard repeats, cooldown taps and buffered movement cannot complete it. Other combat freezes during the short sequence; pause freezes the sequence. After press5, a native frontal image of the olive enemy flies toward the camera, hits the screen, cracks it and slides/falls away. Award exactly one kill and release controls cleanly. Do not teleport the player between physical terrain supports. Avoid repeat duels for the same enemy and allow consecutive enemy encounters.

Use an explicit game.duel state (null or phase/elapsed/taps/enemyId/anchor coordinates) and a focused pure duel module. Phases engage .45s, qte indefinite, throw .48s, impact .18s, slide .95s, recover .2s; pose/visual timings may be refined if native art requires it. Use ordinary input qte boolean with rising-edge counting. Snapshot exposes read-only duel and guard state. Enemy target is hidden from its ordinary sprite only while replaced by the native grapple/throw cinematic.

Main gun still fires for5s, then reloads for1.1s rather than.7s. Add a recognizable magazine/bolt reload sound. Weapon/MIRV/missile effects should be credible, remain audible over Surtr and obey sound/pause lifecycle. Original authored synthesis or verified freely licensed local samples are acceptable; record downloaded licenses/source and package samples in the standalone file.

Approach: focused state machine and native cinematic sprites, integrated with the existing fixed-step engine. A separate scene/physics system would duplicate route handling; a CSS-only video would fail to reflect actual combat state. Existing authorization delegates implementation details to root and permits agents, reviews and same-Site publication; no new design approval is required.
